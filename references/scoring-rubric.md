# Scoring rubric

Use 0-100 and status labels:

- Green 0-19: background risk.
- Yellow 20-39: watch; limited convergence.
- Orange 40-59: elevated; several signals or one material high-weight signal.
- Red 60-79: severe; multiple high-weight categories converge.
- Black 80-100: imminent/active crisis; confirmed hostilities, evacuations, closures, or major official action align.

For this Iran-Israel-US workflow, users may colloquially call 60-79 "orange/red". Keep the official label but explain intensity if needed.

## Suggested weights
- Diplomacy/official posture: 12
- Airspace/NOTAM/aviation: 14
- Maritime/shipping/Hormuz: 12
- Military logistics/posture: 12
- Energy/oil: 10
- Cross-asset stress: 8
- Equity sector rotation: 5
- Cyber/GNSS/AIS/internet: 7
- GEOINT/satellite/fire: 6
- Prediction markets: 4
- Crypto/stablecoins: 3
- Source integrity adjustment: ±7

## Category scoring
- 0: no signal or unavailable.
- 1-3: weak/noisy/single-source.
- 4-6: credible but limited.
- 7-8: strong, corroborated, directly relevant.
- 9-10: official/physical/multi-source signal suggesting imminent or active escalation.

Raise confidence when at least three independent high-weight categories align. Lower confidence when key feeds are blocked or signals are single-source.
