# Indicator taxonomy

Use independent categories and do not raise risk materially from one category alone.

1. Diplomacy and official posture — high weight: ceasefire status, talks, mediators, ultimatums, embassy/citizen warnings, IAEA/UN activity, official military or political language.
2. Airspace, NOTAM and aviation — very high weight: SafeAirspace level, FIR closure, NOTAM number/validity, airline cancellations, ADS-B public patterns, tanker/AWACS/ISR concentration, airport closures.
3. Maritime and shipping — very high weight near Hormuz: UKMTO/MSCIO/MARAD alerts, AIS disruptions, merchant rerouting, tanker rates, war-risk premiums, port notices.
4. Military logistics and posture — very high weight: tanker/cargo aircraft staging, naval deployments, air-defense alerts, reservist/civil defense moves, confirmed satellite imagery.
5. Energy and oil complex — high weight: Brent, WTI, Brent-WTI spread, OVX/proxies, curve/backwardation, LNG/tanker disruption, refinery/cargo delays.
6. Cross-asset stress — medium-high: VIX, MOVE, DXY, gold, Treasury yields, credit spreads, USD/ILS, USD/TRY, EUR/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CHF.
7. Equity sector rotation — medium: defense equities, airlines/tourism, shipping, energy, cyber, US/Europe/Asia/Israel divergence.
8. Crypto and stablecoins — low-medium: BTC/ETH moves, USDT dominance, USDT premium, funding/liquidations if public.
9. Cyber/GNSS/AIS/internet — medium-high: GPSJam, GNSS spoofing/jamming, AIS spoofing, Cloudflare Radar anomalies, BGP disruptions, cable outage reports.
10. Prediction markets — low-medium: Polymarket Iran/general, Manifold; use probabilities, volume, liquidity, change; treat as sentiment/liquidity not evidence.
11. GEOINT and thermal anomalies — high if credible: NASA FIRMS, Zoom Earth, satellite imagery articles; beware old imagery.
12. Source integrity and deception risk — apply always: single-source claims, recycled footage, propaganda, bot amplification, unrelated macro drivers.
